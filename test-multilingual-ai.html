🧪 Testing Instructions

Step-by-Step Testing

  1. Open AI Assistant: Navigate to http://localhost:5174/ai-assistant
  2. Select Language: Click on the language buttons (🇺🇸 English, 🇮🇳 हिंदी, 🇮🇳 मराठी)
  3. Test Voice Recognition: Click "Voice Input" and speak in selected language
  4. Test Text-to-Speech: Click "Voice Demo" to hear AI speak in selected language
  5. Test Chat Interface: Type or speak questions and verify responses in correct language
  6. Test Train Queries: Ask about train 12307 in different languages
  7. Verify Translations: Check that UI elements change language correctly
